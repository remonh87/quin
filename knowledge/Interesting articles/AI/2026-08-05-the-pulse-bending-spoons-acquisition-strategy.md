# The Pulse: Bending Spoons' Acquisition Strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**URL:** https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-bending-spoons-acquisition-strategy/
**Verdict:** MEDIUM

### Core News Story

- **Bending Spoons' Acquisition Strategy**: Bending Spoons acquires struggling companies like Airtable ($1.285B) and Evernote, taking over operations with a fraction of the original staff. Their engineering team rationalized Evernote’s architecture—migrating from a Java 11 monolith with 750 manually provisioned VMs to a cloud-native microservices setup in ~6 months, improving performance and reducing oncall load.

- **Criticisms and Impact**: Post-acquisition, Bending Spoons often raises prices (e.g., Evernote’s Pro plan jumped from $37/year to $250/year) and lays off most original teams. The StreamYard acquisition from Hopin exemplifies this: all staff were let go, and operations were consolidated under a smaller team.

- **Broader Implications**: The article highlights risks of overfunding (e.g., Hopin’s $1B raise leading to eventual liquidation) and contrasts outcomes for fast-growing European startups. Bending Spoons’ approach prioritizes efficiency and profitability but at significant human and customer cost.
