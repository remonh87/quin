# The Conductor Developer

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**URL:** https://martinfowler.com/rachels-ramblings/conductor-developer.html
**Verdict:** MEDIUM

- **Shift in Software Development Paradigm**: AI is transitioning from a productivity tool to an integral part of the development process, with developers increasingly orchestrating multiple AI agents rather than just coding.
- **Human Attention as the New Bottleneck**: With AI handling coding and other tasks, human attention has become the critical limiting factor in software development, requiring developers to manage their focus and energy more effectively.
- **Role of Developers as Conductors**: Developers are evolving into "conductors" who coordinate and manage AI agents, making high-level decisions, ensuring quality, and maintaining system coherence, much like a conductor leads an orchestra.

**Verdict**: **MEDIUM** – The article offers valuable conceptual insights into the evolving role of developers in an AI-assisted environment, but it lacks actionable steps or tools. For a busy developer focused on staying current with AI-assisted engineering, the value is limited to broad perspective shifts rather than immediate, practical takeaways.
