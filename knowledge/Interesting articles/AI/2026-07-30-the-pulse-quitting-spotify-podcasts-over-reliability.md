**Date:** 2026-07-30
**URL:** https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-quitting-spotify-podcasts-over-reliability/

### Core News Story

- **Spotify Podcast Platform Reliability Issues**: Host of *The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast* quit publishing video episodes on Spotify after three major outages in five weeks, including publishing failures and widespread platform downtime. Audio episodes remain available via RSS feed.
- **Broken Trust & Transparency**: Spotify failed to provide promised incident reviews, had errors in their creator portal, and showed no improvement despite repeated complaints. The platform lacked a status page and episode-processing status features available on competitors.
- **AI vs. Reliability Trade-off**: The article suggests Spotify may be prioritizing AI adoption (claiming 73% of PRs are AI-assisted) over core product reliability, raising concerns about "AI psychosis" where innovation outpaces stability.
