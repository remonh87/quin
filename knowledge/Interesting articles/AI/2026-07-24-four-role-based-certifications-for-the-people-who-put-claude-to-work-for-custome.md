# Four role-based certifications for the people who put Claude to work for customers | Claude by Anthropic

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**URL:** https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications
**Verdict:** MEDIUM

- **New Role-Based Certifications**: Anthropic has introduced four role-based certifications for Claude, targeting different roles involved in deploying AI solutions. These include:
  - *Claude Certified Associate: Foundations* (for everyday practical use)
  - *Claude Certified Developer: Foundations* (for engineers building with the Claude API)
  - *Claude Certified Architect: Professional* (for advanced integration and governance)
  - *Claude Certified Architect: Foundations* (for solution architects designing agent systems).

- **Rigorous Validation**: Exams are proctored, identity-verified, and designed by subject matter experts to reflect real-world tasks. Over 36,000 consultants have already been certified across 1,300+ organizations, with major firms like Accenture, Capgemini, and Deloitte committing to certify tens of thousands of professionals.

- **Enterprise Focus**: Certifications aim to give enterprises confidence in partner capabilities, with tiered standing in the Claude Partner Network tied to certified practitioner counts, deployed customers, and public endorsements.

**Verdict**: **MEDIUM** value for a busy software developer focused on AI-assisted engineering. While the *Claude Certified Developer: Foundations* could be useful for validating API and agent development skills, the certifications are more relevant for consultants, architects, or those in enterprise roles. The time investment for certification may not align with immediate development priorities. Focus on hands-on coding and API integration first.
