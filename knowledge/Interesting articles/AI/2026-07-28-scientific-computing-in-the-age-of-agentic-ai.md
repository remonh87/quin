### **Scientific computing in the age of agentic AI – Key Takeaways**

- **AI agents accelerate scientific software development** by automating tedious tasks (e.g., build system modernization, code optimization, language migrations), allowing researchers to focus on high-level goals rather than low-level implementation. Case studies (e.g., cyvcf2, MHCflurry) show **10–50% faster iteration** but require **human validation** for correctness.
- **Human oversight remains critical**—agents struggle with scientific validity, edge cases, and long-term maintenance. Best practices include **external benchmarks, parity checks, and staged feedback loops** to catch errors. Without clear stewardship, even well-intentioned projects risk becoming abandoned or unreliable.
- **Stewardship is the bottleneck**—while agents lower implementation costs, **fragmented rewrites** (e.g., rustar-aligner) risk diluting maintenance efforts. Successful projects either **integrate upstream** (e.g., cyvcf2) or establish **new maintainers early** to ensure durability.
