# How avatarin built a 24/7 retail agent with GPT-Realtime

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**URL:** https://openai.com/index/avatarin
**Verdict:** MEDIUM

- **Core Innovation**: avatarin deployed a 24/7 multilingual retail agent using OpenAI’s GPT‑Realtime that handles natural voice/text conversations, blending speech, text, and visual understanding in low-latency interactions—moving beyond keyword-based chatbots to context-aware guidance.

- **Engineering Trade-offs**: The system uses retrieval-augmented generation to ground responses in accurate product data while maintaining conversational fluidity; conversation flows were co-designed with Yamada Denki’s sales experts to reflect real customer needs and adapt dynamically.

- **Outcome & Vision**: In a two-week pilot, 30,000 users engaged with 92% positive feedback; avatarin now aims for a unified AI agent across all retail touchpoints—web, phone, and in-store—embodying a single brand identity through multimodal intelligence.
